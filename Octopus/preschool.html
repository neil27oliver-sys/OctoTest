The Little Star's Big Hug

"Look up, little one! Can you see the North Star shining bright? It stands still, right in the center of the night sky, like a brave, tiny anchor. Now look at all the thousands of stars dancing around it! The North Star is like you—unique and special. And all the stars spinning around it are all the people in the world! They might be close, or they might be far away, speaking different words and laughing different laughs. But here's the secret: No matter how far apart they move, they are always connected to the same center, the same sky. That North Sky hug reminds us that every person, every family, and every friend, no matter where they are, is part of our big, beautiful human circle. We all shine together!"
